# Hi, I’m Eric.

I work in information security, governance, risk, and compliance. I’m particularly interested in AI governance: how people use AI, what agents can access, and how to make useful tools work responsibly.

I also like learning by making things. Sometimes that means trying a new tool, sometimes it means fixing something, and sometimes it means asking enough questions to understand how it works.

Away from that, I’m into gaming, music, and finding things worth spending time with.

This site is a place to collect some of those interests and share what I learn along the way.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/ericonyourphone), see [what I’m doing now](/now/), or explore [my projects](/projects/).
